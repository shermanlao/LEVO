# Product labels

Admin can print LEVO stickers from the catalog. PDFs are generated from SQLite (the product row and `site_contacts`), not static files.

Print at **100%** (do not scale to fit). Cut on the crop marks. Dies are true millimetre size on A4:

| Die | Size | Typical placement |
|-----|------|-------------------|
| Driver / can | 80 × 50 mm | Inside the can or on the driver |
| Housing | 60 × 30 mm | Side of the luminaire body |
| Mini | 40 × 15 mm | Track adapter or end cap |
| Carton | 100 × 60 mm | Outer box |
| Logo | 50 × 20 mm | Brand mark only — wordmark and slogan, no contact |

Chrome matches the datasheet: LEVO wordmark, **LIGHT EVOLUTION**, black type, SKU in `#E10600`. Empty spec columns are omitted. CE / RoHS are not printed (there is no certification flag on the product).

## SKU label

`GET /api/labels/:slug` → `{product_code}-label.pdf`.

Opens from **Label** on `/admin/products/[id]` (`HelpLink`, new tab). Helper: `getProductLabelUrl(slug)` in [`frontend/src/lib/sqlite-api.ts`](../frontend/src/lib/sqlite-api.ts).

Fields from the product row (`LABEL_SPEC_FIELDS` in [`productSpecs.ts`](../backend-server/src/lib/productSpecs.ts)): wattage, input voltage, source lumen, CCT, CRI, IP, beam, dimming, lifetime. Maker line from `site_contacts`.

Button `helpKey`: `admin.products.label`.

## General (brand) label

`GET /api/labels/general` → `LEVO-label.pdf`.

Brand sticker family: wordmark, slogan, and contact on the larger dies. The **Logo** die is wordmark + slogan only (no address, email, or phone).

Opens from **General label** on `/admin/products`. Helper: `getGeneralLabelUrl()`.

Button `helpKey`: `admin.products.label_general`.
